How to choose and train with Grip Tape

Grip tape for compatible bars and rings when extra surface grip is needed.

How can you use this equipment?

Tape changes the feel of a compatible handle surface. It can be useful on a slippery handle, but it does not replace cleaning and inspecting equipment or progressively training your grip.

Training at different levels

  • Beginner: use only if needed after checking the bar.
  • Intermediate: find a suitable surface feel for your exercise.
  • Advanced: monitor tape wear with repeated use.

What should you check before choosing?

Check compatibility with handle material, application method and hygiene. Never use tape to cover damaged equipment or assume it treats hand pain.

Build and installation details depend on the actual item and location. Confirm specifications with WATHBA before ordering.

Common question: Does grip tape strengthen the hands by itself?

It changes surface feel and may reduce slipping. Strength develops through progressive hanging and pulling.
