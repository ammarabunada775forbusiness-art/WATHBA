How to choose and train with Gymnastic Rings

Wooden gymnastic rings for rows, pulling and upper-body control.

How can you use this equipment?

Rings move, unlike a fixed bar. A beginner can start with feet-on-floor rows and adjust body angle, then progress toward support holds and dips when strength and stability allow.

Training at different levels

  • Beginner: supported rows with feet on the ground.
  • Intermediate: support holds and harder pulling variations.
  • Advanced: ring dips and control skills with an appropriate setup.

What should you check before choosing?

Ask about straps, hanging method and a suitable attachment point. Check height and equal ring length before use; avoid an overhead structure that is not intended to support bodyweight.

Build and installation details depend on the actual item and location. Confirm specifications with WATHBA before ordering.

Common question: Should I start with ring dips?

Supported rows are usually an easier entry point. Ring dips require more strength and control.
