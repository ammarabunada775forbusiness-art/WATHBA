How to choose and use Custom Orders

Custom calisthenics equipment in Jordan sized for your space and training goal. If the catalog does not list the piece you need, send a description, reference photo and space measurements alongside the movements you intend to train. This helps define the equipment before any build is discussed.

Who is it for, and what does it train?

For a site or goal the listed equipment does not fit. A custom request starts with a clear problem and site layout; a reference photo explains an idea but is not a fabrication drawing or proof of capacity.

Muscles and movements

Define the movement first: pulling for back and arms, pushing for chest and triceps, or support and trunk drills. A meaningful program cannot be assigned to an item whose design and function are not yet defined.

Skills to build

Translate a training goal into use, clearance and mounting requirements that can be reviewed before fabrication.

A useful request example, rather than a premature workout

  1. “I need a home pulling station, currently use assistance and need access without jumping.”
  2. Include width, depth, height, site photos and known wall or floor type; a precise personal location is only needed when arranging installation.
  3. Ask to review dimensions, materials, finish, mounting, use, price and delivery scope before agreeing fabrication.

Next comes request review, a clear design and agreed specifications; select training once the equipment is confirmed. No single rep scheme fits every custom request.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sending “make this” with a photo but no site dimensions.
  • Confusing drawing dimensions, final dimensions and body clearance.
  • Agreeing price without defining installation, delivery, checks and finish.

Before use: setup, stability and care

  • Agree a clear drawing or dimensional and functional description before fabrication, identifying what needs site inspection.
  • Specify indoor, outdoor or shared use; requirements do not transfer automatically between these cases.
  • State load ratings, warranty periods and delivery dates only when confirmed for the actual order.

Compare options before ordering

Share width, depth, usable height, floor or wall type, location and whether the unit should be mounted or movable. Dimensions, materials and price are discussed after reviewing the actual request.

Frequently asked questions

What should I send for a custom request?

The movement, a reference image if available, site dimensions and the surface where the equipment would be installed.

Can every idea be manufactured?

Sending an idea does not approve fabrication. Design, materials and site suitability are reviewed first; changes may be needed or the idea may be unsuitable.

Are custom orders only for advanced athletes?

No. Limited space, easier access or station layout may justify customization. The solution is tailored to use rather than an athletic label.

When can I get a final price?

After design, dimensions, site and supply and installation scope are clarified. An unspecified item has no assumed price here.
