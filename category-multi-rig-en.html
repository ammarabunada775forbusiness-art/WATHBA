Multi Rig

A multi-station setup starts with the site layout and the movements it should combine. This category leads to product planning, selection guidance and contact.

Start with the intended use

  • Define required movements and stations before combining equipment designs.
  • Review site dimensions, access, movement routes and spacing between stations.
  • Agree dimensions, mounting and build scope for the actual order; do not infer them from an illustrative photo.
