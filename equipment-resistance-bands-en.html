Equipment guide

Use, progression and selection

Light, medium and heavy resistance bands for assisted pull-ups and warm-ups.

How can you use it?

A securely attached band can reduce part of the load during a pull-up. It can also support a warm-up and other resistance exercises depending on its type. Assistance varies by band and stretch; check attachment before stepping into it.

A starting point for each level

  • Beginner: assisted pulling after learning proper attachment.
  • Intermediate: gradually reduce assistance or use another resistance drill.
  • Advanced: warm-up and resistance variation as needed.

Before you choose

The catalog lists light, medium and heavy resistance. Match resistance to your exercise and extension range, and follow the product's handling instructions before anchoring or stretching near your face.

Is a band essential for a first pull-up?

No. Foot support and assisted rows are other options. A band is one possible progression tool.
