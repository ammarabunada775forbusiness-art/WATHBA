WATHBA / Equipment guide

Use, progression and selection

Hand gripper for hand and forearm work alongside calisthenics.

How can you use it?

A hand gripper trains closing the hand separately from hanging on a bar. It can complement your routine, but it does not replace the back and pulling strength required for pull-ups.

A starting point for each level

  • Beginner: controlled squeezes at a manageable resistance.
  • Intermediate: progress repetitions or resistance when available.
  • Advanced: vary grip training while monitoring forearm fatigue.

Before you choose

Ask about the resistance and size actually available. Select a level you can close with control and without pain; rest if fingers or forearms become irritated.

Will a gripper alone improve my pull-up?

It can complement grip work, while the pull-up also requires pulling, shoulder and trunk training on an appropriate bar.
