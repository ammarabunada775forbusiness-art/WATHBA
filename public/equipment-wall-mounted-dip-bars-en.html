Equipment guide

Use, progression and selection

Wall-mounted dip bars for dip and core training while saving floor space.

How can you use it?

This option attaches to a wall instead of standing on floor bases. It may support holds, dips and knee raises depending on the final setup. Use foot assistance if full dips are too difficult at first.

A starting point for each level

  • Beginner: foot-assisted support after checking installation.
  • Intermediate: controlled-range dips and knee raises.
  • Advanced: harder strength and hold-time progressions.

Before you choose

Ask about the listed standard or custom-built option. Check wall type, mounting, installed height and clearance around the body; not every wall is an appropriate attachment point.

Is this interchangeable with freestanding bars?

It saves floor space but requires suitable wall installation. Compare actual dimensions and stability for your room.
