WATHBA / Equipment guide

Use, progression and selection

Wall-mounted calisthenics pull-up bar for hanging, pulling and core work in Jordan.

How can you use it?

A wall bar creates a station for pull-ups, hangs and knee raises. Supported hangs and assisted pulls can precede a full pull-up. Check the mounting surface, attachment and forward clearance before loading your weight.

A starting point for each level

  • Beginner: supported hang with feet on the floor or a stable step.
  • Intermediate: controlled pull-ups with assistance for extra work.
  • Advanced: harder strength and hanging variations with suitable clearance.

Before you choose

WATHBA lists 60 and 90 cm wall projections. Projection measures distance from the wall; it does not define an athlete's level or guarantee clearance for a skill. Measure available space and ask about mounting for your wall.

Do I need a full pull-up before buying a bar?

No. A properly installed bar can support assisted hanging and progressions toward a first pull-up.
