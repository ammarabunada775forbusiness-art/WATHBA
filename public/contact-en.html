Before you order

Questions before you choose

How do I order?

Choose your equipment and size, then add it to the cart or message us directly. We confirm availability, final price and details on WhatsApp before agreeing on the order.

Are prices and sizes fixed?

Some options depend on size and design. The listed choices are a starting point; confirm actual measurements, specifications and price before ordering.

Can I request a custom setup?

We discuss setups around the available room and intended movements. Send width, depth, height, wall or floor type and a reference image if available.

How do I confirm delivery and installation?

Share your location and the equipment you want. Ask us to confirm collection, delivery or installation arrangements and any associated cost before agreeing.
