How to choose and train with Small Freestanding Parallel Bars

Compact 40 cm freestanding parallel bars for support, push and core drills.

How can you use this equipment?

The listed height is 40 cm. This can suit support drills, push-ups and knee raises, with range depending on the athlete. Start with foot-assisted support before deeper dips and check both bars for stability.

Training at different levels

  • Beginner: foot-assisted supports and easier push-ups.
  • Intermediate: holds, knee raises and more controlled range.
  • Advanced: harder progressions if space and stability permit.

What should you check before choosing?

Measure floor space and the height your intended movement needs. A 40 cm height does not guarantee a full dip for every person; verify actual dimensions and stability before buying.

Build and installation details depend on the actual item and location. Confirm specifications with WATHBA before ordering.

Common question: Can I do a full dip on the small version?

That depends on the bar dimensions, your height and the range you need. Confirm measurements; the taller model may better fit full-height dips.
