How to choose and train with Steel Parallettes with Wooden Grip

Steel-base parallettes with wooden grips for pushing, support holds and L-sits.

How can you use this equipment?

This option combines a steel base with a wooden grip as described in the catalog. Use an even floor for push-ups and support holds. Bend the knees before attempting an L-sit; save planche and handstand work for suitable preparation.

Training at different levels

  • Beginner: supported push-up and brief hold with feet on the ground.
  • Intermediate: full push-up and tuck hold.
  • Advanced: L-sit and balance progressions with adequate preparation.

What should you check before choosing?

Small, medium and large versions are listed. Confirm each size's actual dimensions, base stability and grip fit before ordering. Product size does not automatically define skill level.

Build and installation details depend on the actual item and location. Confirm specifications with WATHBA before ordering.

Common question: Do I need the largest version for advanced training?

Not necessarily. Choose dimensions for your movement and space, then progress the exercise itself.
