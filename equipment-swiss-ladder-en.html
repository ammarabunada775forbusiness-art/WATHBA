How to choose and train with Swiss Ladder

Custom-size wall ladder for hanging, movement and bodyweight drills.

How can you use this equipment?

Wall-mounted rungs enable a range of hangs, knee raises and mobility work depending on actual dimensions. Start with simple controlled movements and leave room around your body.

Training at different levels

  • Beginner: supported hanging or gentle movements near the floor.
  • Intermediate: knee raises and trunk control when installation allows.
  • Advanced: harder progressions within the final design's limits.

What should you check before choosing?

This ladder is sized to the space. Measure usable wall height and width and identify the wall material. Ask about rung spacing and installation before ordering.

Build and installation details depend on the actual item and location. Confirm specifications with WATHBA before ordering.

Common question: Is it the same as a wall pull-up bar?

A ladder offers several rung levels; a pull-up bar is a horizontal pulling station. Select by movement and available space.
