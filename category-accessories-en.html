Accessories

Hand grippers, jump ropes, grip tape and resistance bands serve different needs. Choose the function first, then open the item’s specifications and use guide.

Start with the intended use

  • For hand closing, review the gripper; for handle surface, review grip tape. These are different functions.
  • For timing and movement intervals, open the jump rope and its technique and length guide.
  • For assisted pulling, open resistance bands and the first pull-up guide, checking the actual model and anchor point.
