Parallel & Dip Bars

Compare small and large freestanding bars with wall-mounted dip bars. The main differences are movement clearance, bases and mounting, rather than assigning ability levels by equipment size.

Start with the intended use

  • Small bars: check foot and knee clearance for the movement you can perform without touching the floor.
  • Large bars: compare assembled height, base depth and descent space.
  • Wall bars: review wall material, grip spacing and the available mounting position.
