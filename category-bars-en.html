Pull-up Bars

Compare a wall pull-up bar and monkey bars by pulling movements and traversing space. Open the product for dimensions, then follow its choice and practice guides.

Start with the intended use

  • For pulling in one place, start with the wall bar and compare projection and body clearance.
  • For travel between grips, review monkey-bar space, starting access and descent.
  • Mounting and access shape the choice; a size label does not rank athletic ability.
