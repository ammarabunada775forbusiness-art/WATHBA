Gymnastic Rings

Consider the complete ring system: ring, strap, buckle and anchor point. The product page provides reference dimensions; the Academy guide covers supported starts and progression.

Start with the intended use

  • Start with a suitable anchor point and clear space, then review the ring, strap and buckle together.
  • Compare grip diameter, strap length and adjustment of both sides on the product page.
  • Use the supported ring guide first; owning rings does not mean ring dips suit your current ability.
